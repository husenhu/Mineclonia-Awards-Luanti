# ✦ Awards MCL

**Awards MCL** is an immersive rank and achievement collection mod designed specifically for **Mineclonia** (Luanti engine). It adds a rewarding sense of progression, cosmetic flair, and an MMORPG-style collection interface for your players!

---

## ✨ Features

- **XP-Level Rank Medals:** Automatically tracks player experience levels and assigns prestigious tier medals (from Bronze up to Eternal).
- **Head & Chat Badges:** Floats a crisp bitmap medal above each player's head that smoothly follows their movement, paired with colored custom nametags and chat tags (`[Level Tier] Name`).
- **MMORPG-Style "Award Collection" GUI:** Players can browse all available medals in a sleek dark-mode grid UI, inspecting locked/unlocked statuses, requirements, and dynamic tag previews.
- **Quick Inventory Access:** Features an elegant trophy button integrated directly into the player inventory next to the character model for instant access[cite: 4].
- **Monster Slayer Statistics:** Kills of Creepers, Witches, Zombies, Skeletons and Spiders (including variants like Husk, Stray and Cave Spider) are counted per family plus an overall total, persisted in player meta. Each family has 6 trophy tiers (Bronze → Legendary) viewable in the "Monster Slayer" tab of the Award Collection GUI, with tier-up announcements in chat.
- **Rank-Up Celebrations:** Triggers celebratory sound effects, global chat announcements, and stunning particle fireworks upon ranking up.

---

## 🎮 Commands

- **/rank** — Check your current rank, level, and progress toward the next tier.
- **/collection** — Open the Award Collection UI manually.
